I made my first merged open-source contribution to QDash by fixing a tooltip that could disappear when users zoomed out. My change helps people read information on the screen without it getting cut off.

I found the issue while looking for a real project where I could learn and contribute. My first solution used custom code, but review feedback showed me that the project already had a better tool for the job.

I changed my approach, reused the existing component, and added tests to check the behavior. I also ran the project's full checks before submitting the final version.

A maintainer reviewed, approved, and merged my pull request.

This experience taught me to look for existing solutions before building new ones. It also showed me how useful feedback can be when I take the time to understand it and improve my work.
